The Product

Vibes Mesh — not a player.
A feeling-to-music engine.

Six axes. Each science-backed. Each orthogonal — moving one does not imply movement on another. Slayer has max Energy and near-zero Groove. Phoebe Bridgers and death metal share low Energy but are opposite on Emotion. This is the decomposition that makes it work.

Interactive — pick a preset or explore
This IS the product. The shape you see is a vibe vector in 6D space. Cosine similarity finds songs whose vectors point in the same direction. 114K songs pre-vectorized. Zero runtime audio analysis. <200ms query.
51%
Familiar — songs they know

The handshake. Says "this system understood your feeling." Familiarity is load-bearing — it earns trust before discovery can work.

49%
Discovery — songs that deserve to be heard

Quality floor: popularity ≥ 35 (real people have heard and responded, not zero-play uploads). The discovery happens quietly — no badge, no announcement. Just music that fits.
