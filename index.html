The science behind it
Every axis cross-examined against peer-reviewed music psychology research. Divergences are justified, not apologized for.
⚡ EnergyParallel
Weightless→Full rage
Arousal — the single most replicated dimension in all of music psychology. Validated across 40+ years of research and multiple cultures.
Brian Eno (0.05) · Slayer (0.95)
🎵 GrooveParallel
Static→Can't sit still
Contemporary/rhythmic factor (MUSIC model, Rentfrow). Orthogonal to Energy — the Slayer test confirmed these axes are fully independent.
Slayer: Energy 0.95, Groove 0.10. D'Angelo: Energy 0.30, Groove 0.80.
📻 FidelityJustified divergence
Hi-fi polished→Lo-fi abrasive
Science bundles this inside genre proxies. We decompose it as its own sonic dimension. Lo-fi hip hop and lo-fi indie share texture across genres. The vocabulary is already culturally fluent.
Michael Bublé (0.05) · Nine Inch Nails (0.90)
🎤 VocalsJustified divergence
Instrumental→Raw exposed
Science identified this confound in the Sophisticated music factor and never resolved it. We resolve it — presence and character as one clean axis.
Tycho (0.05) · Fred Again — textural (0.40) · Phoebe Bridgers (0.85)
🌗 EmotionParallel
Dark / anguished→Euphoric
Valence. Russell's circumplex model. 40+ years of research. Cross-culturally consistent (PNAS 13-dimension study). Nearly dropped mid-session — now flagged in the codebase: never drop this.
Phoebe Bridgers (0.15) · Karol G Tusa (0.90)
👥 SocialNovel · Our IP
3am headphones→Room full of people
Science studies listener social context. Nobody has encoded it as a property of the music itself. Artists build music intentionally for a context. We encode that intention.
Aphex Twin ambient (0.05) · Karol G Tusa (0.95)
